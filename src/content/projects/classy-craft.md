- Classes, interfaces and abstract classes connected by association, generalization and dependency.
- Projects that group several diagrams; import and export as UML files or images.
- **Command** for undo and redo, **State** for selecting, drawing and moving, **Composite** for nested elements, **Observer** for keeping views in sync, **Factory Method** for creating diagram elements.
