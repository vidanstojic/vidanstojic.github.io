- **Model-View-Intent** for predictable, unidirectional state.
- **Room** for cached breed data and **DataStore** for the local user profile created at first launch.
- **Hilt** for dependency injection and **Coroutines** for background work.
- Breed list with search, detail screens, and a gallery with a full-screen photo viewer.
