## Rules and balance (October 2026)

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cheap cards beat stars | No limit on cards, so quantity won | Default rules: five cards each and a paid hand-over |
| Scout won 9 matches in 10 | Exact ratings plus a perfect lookahead | Guesses within 2 points, cautious in classic rules |
| Bots overpaid for wrong ingredients | Fair share counted every card | Fair share counts only useful cards |
| Wrong ingredients fell on whoever opened | The opener always had to bid | In food the opener may skip a card |
| Split screen showed a thin strip of pitch | Fixed-height surface | The surface fills the screen, one half per player |

## First tester round (November 2026)

| Feedback | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| "Outbid your rival." was cut off on the home screen | Taller table and smaller cards, so the tagline fits even with large fonts |
| Bots talked about strikers in a movie match | Taglines per theme |
| The basketball timer was hard to read | The same countdown ring as in every other theme |
| No way to pause | Pause menu with Resume and Resign; the match also pauses when the app goes to the background |
| One player ended with far more cards (251:581) | Same number of cards for both players, on by default |
| Results like 400:250 don't look like football | A realistic score from the rating gap per card |
| The same players kept coming back | A shuffle bag per theme, saved across restarts |
| New players didn't understand the game | A four-page How to play guide that opens on the first launch |
| The game was silent | Music, sound effects and vibration, each with its own switch |
| The phone buzzed after your own bid | A short buzz when the turn comes to you instead |

## Getting ready for Google Play

- Release builds show the version on the home screen, so testers can say which build they played.
- "Play a friend" shows as coming soon until the server exists.
- Signed release builds, store listing, screenshots and the privacy policy for closed testing.
- *Tactical Rounds* became the match music.
