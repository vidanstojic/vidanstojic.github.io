_Effective: November 17, 2026_

Draft Duel ("the app") is a game developed by Vidan Stojić, an individual developer in Belgrade, Serbia. This policy explains what the app does with information about you and your device.

## Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for an account, does not collect or share personal information, and does not use analytics, crash reporting or advertising services. It does not request internet access: matches against bots and on the same device run entirely on your phone, and nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

## Information stored on your device

To work properly the app saves a few things in its private storage on your phone:

- your settings: music, sound effects and vibration on or off;
- which cards you have already met, so the next matches do not repeat them;
- whether you have seen the How to play guide.

This information never leaves your device. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data in your phone's settings. The match you are playing and the rules you chose are kept in memory only while the app is open.

## Permissions

The app asks for one permission: **vibration**, used for short haptic feedback during a match (for example when it is your turn). You can turn vibration off in the app's settings.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If a later version adds online play, accounts, crash reporting or analytics, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
