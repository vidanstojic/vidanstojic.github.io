This page explains what vidanstojic.me collects when you visit it. In short: no cookies, no tracking across websites and nothing that tells me who you are. The privacy policies of my apps are listed at the end.

## Visit statistics

The site counts visits with [Umami](https://umami.is), a privacy-focused analytics service. Umami does not use cookies, does not follow you across websites and does not collect your name, email address or anything else that identifies you.

What is recorded, anonymously and only as totals:

- the page you open and the site that sent you here;
- your country, browser, operating system and type of device;
- a few actions: opening or downloading the CV, sending a message, opening a gallery, switching the language, and clicking a GitHub, LinkedIn or Google Play link.

If your browser sends a Do Not Track signal, your visit is not counted. Statistics are kept for up to six months.

## Contact form

When you send a message, your name, email address, topic and message are delivered to my inbox by [Web3Forms](https://web3forms.com), a form delivery service. I use them only to reply to you. Ask me and I will delete your message.

## CV builder

A custom CV is put together entirely in your browser. The projects you pick are not sent anywhere.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. To deliver the pages, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address under its own privacy statement. Fonts and every other file come from this site, with no third-party font or advertising services.

## Cookies

This site sets no cookies.
